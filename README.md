# Cab Go Zimbabwe

WhatsApp Taxi. No app. No data. Just WhatsApp.

Gogo saves our number, sends "Nkulumane 5 to town" or a voice note.
Bot sends price. Drivers see ride. Driver calls gogo.

### Price

Incity: $1.50 per 2km + $0.50 driver fee if under 10km, $1.00 if over 10km.
Outside tollgate: $1.50 per 2km + Driver fee up to driver.

Example: Nkulumane 5 to CBD, 5km = $5.00
Example: Bulawayo to Gweru, 160km = $120.00 + driver fee

### How it works

1. Customer sends pickup and dropoff on WhatsApp
2. Bot sends price
3. Bot sends ride to Driver Group
4. After 60 seconds, bot sends driver list to customer
5. Customer picks 1, 2 or 3
6. Driver gets customer number after 2 minutes and calls
